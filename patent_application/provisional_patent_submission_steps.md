# Steps to File a US Provisional Patent Application

This guide walks you through preparing and submitting a **provisional patent application** with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO).

---

## 1. Understand the Purpose
A **provisional application**:
- Secures an early filing date.
- Is not examined by the USPTO.
- Expires after **12 months** (must file a non-provisional before expiration to keep your priority date).
- Allows you to label your invention as "Patent Pending".

---

## 2. Prepare the Application Materials

### Required Components
1. **Cover Sheet** (USPTO Form SB/16)
   - Inventor(s) name, address, and contact information.
   - Title of the invention.
   - Correspondence address.
   - Attorney/agent information (if applicable).
   - U.S. Government interest (if any).
   - Payment method.

2. **Written Description**  
   - Detailed description of your invention (as in your robust provisional application draft).  
   - Include enough detail for someone skilled in the field to reproduce the invention.

3. **Drawings/Figures** (if applicable)
   - Flowcharts, block diagrams, architecture drawings, etc.
   - Should be labeled and referenced in the written description.

4. **Filing Fee**
   - Current USPTO fee schedule (as of 2025):  
     - Micro entity: $60  
     - Small entity: $120  
     - Large entity: $300  
   - Micro entity status can significantly reduce fees.
   - I qualify for "Micro" status.

---

## 3. Create a USPTO Account
- Go to [USPTO Patent Center](https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/).
- Register for a **USPTO.gov** account.
- Verify identity if needed (for future non-provisional filings).

---

## 4. Prepare Files for Submission
- Save your **application text** as a PDF.
- Save any **drawings** as PDF or in a single PDF with the written description.
- Prepare your **cover sheet** as a PDF.
- Have payment information ready (credit card, deposit account, or EFT).

---

## 5. File Online via USPTO Patent Center
1. Log in to [Patent Center](https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/).
2. Select **File New Application** → **Provisional Application**.
3. Upload:
   - Written description (PDF)
   - Drawings (PDF)
   - Cover sheet (Form SB/16)
4. Enter filing details:
   - Title of invention
   - Inventor information
   - Correspondence address
5. Review and confirm entity status (micro, small, or large).
6. Pay the filing fee.
7. Submit the application.

---

## 6. Receive Filing Receipt
- After submission, the USPTO will generate an **Electronic Acknowledgement Receipt** with:
  - Application number
  - Filing date
  - Confirmation number
- Save this in your records — it’s proof of filing.

---

## 7. Mark Your Invention as "Patent Pending"
- You can now mark related materials, websites, or products with **“Patent Pending”**.
- Do not disclose additional details publicly beyond what’s in the provisional unless you’re comfortable losing secrecy.

---

## 8. Plan for the Non-Provisional
- A provisional expires after 12 months.
- Before expiration, decide whether to:
  - File a **non-provisional patent application** (claims priority to the provisional).
  - File multiple non-provisionals if your provisional covers more than one invention.

---

## 9. Keep Your Documents Organized
Maintain a file with:
- Your provisional PDF
- Drawings
- Cover sheet
- Filing receipt
- USPTO correspondence
