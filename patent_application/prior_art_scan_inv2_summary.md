# Prior Art Scan — Summary (Invention 2: Adaptive, Goal-Directed Learning Framework)

**Scope:** Focused on invention 2 — adaptive course engine using transitive prerequisite navigation, mastery gating, multi-path goal progression, and targeted re-testing of incorrect items.

---

## Key Findings

- **Prerequisite graphs**, **goal-directed pathing**, and **mastery-based gating** are common in granted patents and published applications.
- **Targeted re-testing of only previously incorrect items** is rarely explicit — could be a differentiating element.
- Few references combine **all four features** in a single framework.

---

## Representative References

1. **US 10,490,092 B2 (2019)** — Mastery learning platforms with knowledge maps; no explicit targeted re-testing.
2. **US 7,052,277 B2 (2006)** — Early adaptive sequencing; no re-test targeting.
3. **US 11,403,565 B2 (2022)** — Learning paths from structured graphs; lacks targeted re-testing.
4. **US 2019/0226868 A1** — Goal/prereq graphs for adaptive learning; re-testing not specified.
5. **US 2022/0327946 A1** — Skills hierarchy tracking; re-testing logic not described.

---

## Novelty Opportunities

- Emphasize formal readiness functions with mastery tiers and transitive prerequisite enforcement.
- Define goal-path cost functions optimizing for learning efficiency.
- Claim targeted re-testing as a systematic step tied to readiness status.
