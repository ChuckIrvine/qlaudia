# Prior Art Scan — Summary (Invention 1: AI-Populated Unified Course KB Schema via Generic AI Prompts)

**Core Concept:**  
A standardized, course-specific knowledge base schema that is automatically populated **entirely by an AI agent** using **generic, subject-agnostic prompts**. The AI generates all elements (topics, prerequisites, study content, examples, and assessments), with validation and deployment performed automatically.

---

## Key Novelty Emphasis
- **Generic AI prompts** — prompts are *subject-agnostic*, meaning the same prompt templates can be applied to any academic or training domain to produce structured course content.
- **AI-only content derivation** — all course material is generated by AI; no manual authoring of learning content is required.
- **Canonical schema** — fixed field definitions (topics, prerequisites, content, examples, quizzes) that are filled entirely by AI output.
- **Validation pipeline** — AI outputs are checked for schema compliance, logical consistency (e.g., prerequisite chains), and pedagogical soundness before deployment.
- **Plug-and-play deployment** — validated KBs can be deployed directly into a compatible learning engine without further human intervention.

---

## Key Findings from Prior Art Scan
- Most prior art uses AI or algorithms to **assist** human authors, not to fully auto-generate course KBs from generic prompts.
- No surveyed references disclose:
  - A **course-specific canonical schema** populated end-to-end by AI.
  - Use of **generic prompts** applicable to any domain.
  - Automated prerequisite validation combined with deployment to an LMS-ready format.
- Related art exists in knowledge graphs, adaptive systems, and general AI document generation, but lacks the above combination.

---

## Representative References
1. **US 11,645,095 B2 (2023)** — Builds tutorial knowledge graphs; no schema standardization or prompt-based AI generation.
2. **US 2023/0351102 A1** — AI document generation; not course-specific, no canonical schema.
3. **US 2023/0259705 A1** — Structured data transformations; no AI generation pipeline from prompts.
4. **US 10,490,092 B2 (2019)** — Mastery learning platforms; does not describe AI-generated KBs.

---

## Novelty Opportunities
- Claim **generic prompt templates** as a core method step.
- Claim **AI-only population** of all schema fields.
- Claim **schema validation rules** that are independent of subject matter.
- Claim **immediate deployment capability** without human content editing.
