# Patent Strategy Analysis

## Overview
Your invention description contains at least **two distinct, independently valuable inventions**:

---

## Invention 1 — AI-Populated Unified Data Structure for Online Courses

**Core inventive concept:**  
A **specific, unified Knowledge Base (KB) schema** for representing a course, populated via AI queries, containing topics, prerequisites, examples, quizzes, and other instructional components.

**Scope:**  
Can be used in *any* course framework — yours or another party's — so long as they generate and populate the data structure using an AI in your described way.

**Claim focus:**  
- The data structure itself (as a computer-readable medium).
- The method of generating it with an AI.
- Systems that use this method.

**Independence:**  
Does not depend on your adaptive learning framework to have value.

---

## Invention 2 — Adaptive, Goal-Directed Course Framework

**Core inventive concept:**  
A learning engine that uses **transitive prerequisite navigation** to provide multiple learning paths, including goal-oriented progression and readiness enforcement.

**Scope:**  
Works with any compatible course content, regardless of how it was generated.

**Claim focus:**  
- The method of dynamically filtering, sequencing, and recommending topics based on mastery and prerequisites.
- The system architecture for goal-directed adaptive learning.

**Independence:**  
Does not require the AI-populated KB — could use manually created or third-party content.

---

## Filing Strategy Options

### Option A — One Patent Application, Multiple Claim Sets
Include two independent claim families in one filing:
1. KB generation/data structure.
2. Adaptive learning engine.

**Pros:**
- Single filing fee and drafting cost initially.
- One priority date for both.

**Cons:**
- USPTO may issue a restriction requirement, forcing you to choose one to prosecute and file a divisional for the other later.

---

### Option B — Separate Applications from the Start
File one app for the KB schema and one for the adaptive framework.

**Pros:**
- Each gets its own prosecution path.
- Avoids restriction delays.
- Easier to license/sell separately.

**Cons:**
- Higher upfront cost.

---

## Recommended Hybrid Approach
- File **one provisional** covering both inventions in detail.
- Within 12 months, decide whether to file:
  - One non-provisional with two claim families, or
  - Two separate non-provisionals.

This secures the **earliest priority date** for both inventions without the cost of two full filings upfront.
