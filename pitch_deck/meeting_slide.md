## Slide 1 — Education Is the Largest Untransformed Market on Earth

**Headline:** $7.3 trillion a year is spent on education and training, and the course itself is still made by hand.

Global education and training spend, 2025. Source: HolonIQ.

> **Speaker notes:**
> **Say it in thirty seconds, then play the video.** *"Education is a $7.3 trillion market, and its product, the course, is still made entirely by hand. That is what I'm changing. Here's what it looks like."*
>
> **If someone says healthcare is bigger:** it is ($9.8T, 2022, WHO). The claim is about the largest market that is still *untransformed*: nobody has made the core product, a coherent, sequenced body of knowledge, in any way other than by hand.
>
> **If someone says AI is already changing education:** most of it changes how courses are delivered: tutors, video, quizzes. The content is still written by hand. Qlaudia generates the course itself.
>
> **Don't linger.** No tables and no market-share arithmetic. If anyone wants the numbers, the full market slide is in the pitch deck.
